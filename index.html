Marcos Lage

mlage@ic.uff.br

I'm an Associate Professor at the Instituto de Computação at UFF, having held the position of Director between 2021 and 2024. I earned my B.Sc. and Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics at PUC-Rio and completed postdoctoral research at New York University in 2015 and 2018, focusing on Urban Data Visualization. I am a Productivity Fellow of CNPq (level C) and a FAPERJ Scientist.

I served as SIBGRAPI's general chair in 2017, program chair in 2019 and Computers & Graphics chair in 2025. Currently, I am an Associate Editor of the Journal of the Brazilian Computer Society. I was a member of the Special Interest Group on Computer Graphics, Pattern Recognition and Image Processing of the Brazilian Computer Society in 2020-2021 and 2024-2025.

I have published over 50 articles in high-impact journals and conferences, including IEEE TVCG, Computers & Graphics, and IEEE VIS, with award-winning papers presented at international events. My research on data visualization has been featured in outlets such as The Economist and The New York Times.
